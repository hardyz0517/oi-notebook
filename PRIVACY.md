# Privacy

OI Notebook is designed as a local desktop workspace. Notes are saved locally by default.

AI features may send the necessary prompt, selected note context, or related task content to the AI provider configured by the user.

Web search and Research Engine features may send search keywords or related query text to public search services or configured providers.

Luogu Cookie values are used only for Luogu-related features configured by the user, such as reading Luogu pages or scanning submissions. OI Notebook does not read browser cookies or system login state.

Avoid sending private notes, passwords, API keys, cookies, or other secrets to AI or external services unless you intentionally choose to do so.
